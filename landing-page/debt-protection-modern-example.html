Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

What is a Debt Protection Plan?
It’s a monthly subscription product that cancels your monthly loan payments if an unexpected covered event occurs — protecting you and your loan, and giving you peace of mind.
Will buying this affect my loan or my credit?
No. The Plan is optional — whether or not you purchase it will not affect your application for credit or the terms of any existing credit agreement you have with your credit union.
How do I make a claim?
You work directly with your financial institution to get your benefits.
Can I cancel?
Yes — there’s no long-term commitment and you may cancel the Plan at any time. A deductible may apply.
Who’s behind the coverage?
Debt protection is a product of the lender. Minnesota Life Insurance Company — a Securian Financial company — acts as the administrator of the lender’s debt protection program. See the Plan Agreement for details, including eligibility requirements, conditions, and exclusions.
How do I find out what it costs?
Your price depends on your loan, so the fastest way is your personalized quote — it takes just a few minutes online. Get my quote.

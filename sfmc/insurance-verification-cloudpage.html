Why are we asking?

When you financed with %%=v(@lenderName)=%%, you agreed to keep insurance on the vehicle or property that secures your loan. We periodically confirm that coverage is still active — it protects you, and it protects the collateral.

If we can't confirm coverage, insurance may be purchased on your behalf and added to your loan — and it's usually more expensive than your own policy. Verifying now is the easiest way to avoid that.
